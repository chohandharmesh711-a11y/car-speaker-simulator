About This Simulation

This simulator uses scientifically-tuned acoustic profiles for real Indian cars. Each car model has been configured with accurate speaker placement, cabin acoustics, frequency response, and bass characteristics. The seat position affects how you experience the audio based on speaker distance and positioning.
